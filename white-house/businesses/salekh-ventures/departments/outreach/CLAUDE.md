# salekh-ventures / outreach

The Outreach room on the Salekh Ventures floor: prospecting for the studio's first clients. Salekh's brief and the strategy are in this floor's `knowledge/` folder.

## What this room does today

Finds businesses that match the settings, analyses each from its own public pages and Companies House, filters and scores them by rule, and drafts a card for each that passes. The result is one shortlist a day for Salekh. He messages and calls them himself.

## What it never does

- It sends one thing only: a short test enquiry by email, from the test mailbox, to a business's published address, and only after Salekh has approved that exact message. One per business, ever. Nothing else is sent to anyone: no outreach message, no follow up, no reply, no form, no call.
- A test enquiry never books an appointment, never describes a health problem, and says nothing false about who is asking.
- It never reads a LinkedIn profile. Public job adverts only.
- It uses public business information only. Nothing about patients or any individual customer of a prospect. No ROOTS data of any kind.
- It never contacts or lists a business on the suppression list.

## Facts the drafts may use about us

- Salekh Ventures builds AI agents to order for a business, each doing one job.
- Every action an agent takes is approved, logged and can be stopped.
- Salekh runs his own businesses on the same system.
