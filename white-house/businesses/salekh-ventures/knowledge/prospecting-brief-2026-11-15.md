# Prospecting agent: Salekh's brief, 15 Nov 2026

Salekh's own words, lightly tidied. Top priority: this is how the first 10 clients are found. Strategy is in `strategy-2026-11-15.md` beside this file.

## The goal

Every morning by 9am Salekh gets a shortlist of about 20 businesses worth contacting that day, each with proof of a problem we can fix and a drafted message. He messages and calls them himself. The agent does everything before that.

## First target

Private dental and aesthetics clinics, West Midlands first, then other UK cities. The type of business and the area are settings: trades, solicitors and others come later.

## The pipeline, run daily

1. **Find** about 100 businesses a day. Signals: advertising for a receptionist or front desk role, running Meta ads, slow or dead social accounts, poor or unanswered reviews. Sources: public job ads, business websites, Google Maps, Meta Ad Library, Companies House. Never contact the same business twice. One master list.
2. **Analyse** each: what they sell and their highest value service, rough size (staff, locations), whether they run ads, review count and rating, how a customer can enquire (form, WhatsApp, Instagram, email, phone), owner or manager name, whether it is a limited company.
3. **Test** each (mystery enquiry). One short, realistic customer enquiry, and the same to three local competitors. Never book an appointment, never give false medical details, one enquiry per business, no follow up from the test identity. Record time sent, time of first reply, whether they followed up, the reply text. Wait 24 hours before scoring.
4. **Filter.** Keep a business only if it replied slowly or not at all, clearly has money (high value services, ads running, or several staff), is a limited company, and has a named person to contact. Fast repliers are not prospects: store them as the comparison for others in the same area.
5. **Daily shortlist**, one card per business: name, area, website, owner or manager, every contact route; what they sell and why they made the list; the test result; the fastest local competitor's result; the reply our agent would have sent, written for their business; a drafted first message for Salekh to send, which states their result and says plainly that the enquiry was a test; a one line call opener; a score, best first.
6. **Follow ups**, drafted for approval: day 3 one thing a competitor does better; day 7 a 30 second screen recording of the agent handling their enquiry; day 14 close out.
7. **Replies.** Every reply from a prospect goes straight to Salekh with a drafted response. Opt outs are honoured automatically and permanently.

## Approvals

Week 1: Salekh approves every enquiry template and every outgoing message. Weeks 2 to 3: a sample of ten a day plus anything the agent is unsure about. After that: only replies, complaints and odd cases. Uses the existing rungs and gateway. Nothing is sent to a prospect by the agent until he moves it up a rung.

## Volume

Finding, analysing and testing: 100 a day from the start if the accounts allow. Outgoing outreach: 25 a day, reaching 100 by week three.

## Tracking

Every business found, tested, filtered out (with the reason), shortlisted, messaged, replied, call booked and paid. Numbers weekly.

## Hard rules

- Public business information only. No scraping LinkedIn profiles.
- Limited companies only for cold email.
- No ROOTS or patient data anywhere near this.
- Everything goes through the gateway and the audit log.
