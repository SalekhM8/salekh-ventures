## What we sell

A shop of AI agents, set up to order for any business that can afford it. The reason to trust it over other AI sellers: every action is approved, logged and can be stopped, and Salekh runs his own businesses on it.

- The shop shows every agent, grouped by department, each marked "live" or "built to order".
- Each outreach message leads with one agent, chosen by a signal about that prospect.

| Signal | Lead agent |
| --- | --- |
| Advertising for a receptionist or admin | Enquiries |
| Running Meta ads | Ads with data |
| Dead or thin social accounts | Content |

Sellable by January: enquiries, content, ads with data, research. Everything else is built to order.

## Changed 15 Nov after reading first-hand accounts on Reddit

Source: ten threads from people running or hiring AI automation agencies (text in `transcripts/reddit-*.txt`). Single unverified accounts, two of them ending in a product plug, and four had the main post removed. Treated as stronger than guru posts and weaker than data.

1. **Cold outreach goes out as one trade plus one job, never "AI for your business".** A UK seller reported nothing from "AI automation for small businesses" and calls from "AI voice receptionist for plumbers". The shop stays broad; it is shown after the first sale.
2. **Partners move to first place.** One seller made 465 calls and hundreds of emails to trades for zero revenue, then switched to selling through marketing agencies that already had the relationships. Find them from "website by" footers on trade and clinic sites.
3. **Trades are not reached by email.** Use Facebook, WhatsApp and partners for trades. Keep email for clinics, estate agents, accountants and other office-based firms.
4. **The preview page is tested small before it is built big.** The same seller made a personalised demo page per prospect and nobody opened one. First 100 sends: if under 10 in 100 open it, the proof goes inside the message and the page is dropped. The live demo happens on the call.
5. **Clients get a WhatsApp summary, not a dashboard.** One seller built a dashboard no client logged into; another lost three clients who "couldn't see what they were paying for". So results are pushed to the client each week where they already are, with approvals by reply. The 3D house is for marketing and demos.
6. **Live within seven days of signing.** Reported pattern: no value by week two and the client goes quiet.
7. **Founding price is locked for three months, not a year.** One seller regretted twelve-month contracts at a case-study price.
8. **Expectation.** The accounts read: first paying client after four months; two to ten clients in eight months with two people. Ten by 8 February stays the target; plan cash on three to five.

## Offer

1. Free automated preview: their agent built from their public website, and their floor in the 3D house. Labelled a preview.
2. A call.
3. Paid first month plus setup fee. Refunded if the agent is not live and hitting the agreed numbers in 30 days. Void if the client does not supply access, leads or approvals.

Added 15 Nov from the saved-post review:
- The preview asks two or three questions (enquiries a month, what they would hand off, team size) so each prospect qualifies themselves.
- Onboarding is built so the client sees a result in their first three days.
- Every message and video opens with a line about the reader and their problem, said to one person, with nothing that cannot be checked.

No free trials, except two or three strategic names in exchange for a case study and introductions.

Tripwire: if the first ten calls produce nobody willing to pay upfront, bring back a seven day free run for prospects who have given access and named a decision date.

## Price (to be tested on calls)

- Single agent: about £250 to £500 a month.
- Bundle of three or more: £1,500 to £5,000 a month.
- Setup fee on every sale. Higher when the agent has to be built.
- Never guarantee sales or a return. Guarantee delivery numbers only.

## How we reach people

Run by the White House itself, with Salekh approving batches. This is also the content: "I used my AI to get clients for my AI".

1. Find: limited companies with a signal (job ads, Meta Ad Library, Google Maps, Companies House). No LinkedIn scraping.
2. Research: the Research agent reads the site and picks the lead agent, three specifics and one competitor.
3. Preview link generated.
4. Message drafted. Salekh approves the day's batch.
5. Sequence: day 0 job application or preview, day 3 competitor comparison, day 7 mystery shop result, day 14 close out.
6. Replies drafted by the agent, approved by Salekh, booking link sent.

Channels: email for volume, Facebook messages for trades, Instagram messages for clinics, contact forms, LinkedIn by hand. No cold phone calls. Calls only with people who replied.

Daily target from December: about 150 touches (estimate of safe limits, not platform rules).

## Numbers we are working to (estimates, not benchmarks)
