## What it is

- Multi-channel stock sync for UK sellers who sell on two or more marketplaces. One catalogue, one stock count, every marketplace. Site: mastorehq.com. Company: Salekh Ventures Ltd.
- In its own words: "Manage everything from one place." "List once. Sell everywhere." "Pay for your orders, not your channels."

## The offer (from the pricing page and plan code)

- Starter £25 a month or £250 a year: up to 150 orders a month, 1 login.
- Growth £49 or £490: up to 1,000 orders, adds the Sales page and running Mastore from Claude or ChatGPT.
- Pro £99 or £990: unlimited orders, 5 team logins.
- 14 day trial of Growth, no card needed. Yearly is two months free. No cut of sales.
- Own-website connection: £300 one-off on the Pro plan, sold by email, built by hand each time.
